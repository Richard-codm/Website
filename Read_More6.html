More on our Vehicle Trade-In & Valuation

Selling your car privately often involves tedious paperwork, unreliable buyers, and frustrating low-ball offers. At Owie Car Dealership, our Vehicle Trade-In & Buyout service provides a fast, safe, and lucrative alternative. Whether you want to trade in your current vehicle toward an upgrade from our inventory or simply want to sell your car for competitive cash, we make the entire transaction smooth and stress-free. Our valuation process is grounded in honesty and current market accuracy. When you bring your car in, our expert appraisers conduct a thorough, objective assessment examining its mechanical condition, exterior presentation, mileage, and historical market demand. We utilize industry-standard valuation technology to ensure our offer reflects true top-market value. Within minutes, you receive a firm, written cash offer that you can accept on the spot. If you decide to trade in, we seamlessly transfer your vehicle's appraisal value directly toward your new down payment, reducing your overall loan amount and lowering your monthly payments. If you owe money on your current vehicle's loan, our team manages all the paperwork and bank pay-offs on your behalf. Best of all, our buyout offer carries zero obligation—if you decide to sell us your car without buying one of ours, we will write you a check with no pressure attached.
